Origin of Triple T (this is header two btw)

Tung Tung Tung Sahur is an AI generated meme that first appeared in February 2025.

The meaning behind him starts in his name. "Tung tung tung" is an onomotopeia, it describes the sound made by a kentongan, a traditional indonesian wooden drum used to wake up villagers or give alerts.

"Sahur", in Islamic tradition, is the pre-dawn meal you eat before fasting during Ramadan. So the connection between both things is the drum is used to wake people up for sahur before fasting.

It all starts in the morning during Ramadan, if you are called for sahur three times, and you do not answer, Tung Tung Tung Sahur will appear. Nobody has survived to tell the tale of what Tung Tung Tung sahur does if you do not answer the calls for sahur.

This wooden anomaly first appeared alongside its other AI generated companions and paved the way for many other creations. One may know the other popular anomilies as "Tralalero Tralala" and "Ballerina Cappuccina".
